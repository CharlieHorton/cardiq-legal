# Support

<p class="lede">Something not working, or a price that looks wrong? Get in touch. We read everything.</p>

## Contact

Email [charliehorton.dev@gmail.com](mailto:charliehorton.dev@gmail.com?subject=CardIQ%20support). If a price looks wrong, include the card's name, set and number.

## What are the game modes?

- **Daily Challenge:** five cards, the same for everyone. It resets at midnight UK time, and each guess is locked in before the price is revealed.
- **Classic:** ten cards. The closer your guess, the more you score.
- **Price Range:** pick the price band. A guess one band out still scores.
- **Rank It:** order four cards from most to least valuable.
- **Higher or Lower:** is the next card worth more or less? One wrong call ends the run.
- **Pick the Chase:** three cards. Tap the most valuable one.

Three themed packs rotate every Monday. The Screener lets you filter by era, Pokémon, set or price, then deal a game of any mode from what's left.

## How is my guess scored?

Every card is worth up to 1,000 points. The closer your guess is to the market price, the more you score. A guess within 20% still earns most of the points, and a bullseye within 2% earns nearly all of them.

## Where do the prices come from?

Prices are UK market values from PokePulse, refreshed daily. Each reveal also shows US and Cardmarket prices where available. Once enough history has built up, it also shows which way the price has moved.

## What is Market IQ?

A rating out of 1,000 that measures how accurately you price cards. It measures skill, not how much you play. It tracks your recent guesses, so it rises and falls as you improve.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently removes your account and everything synced to it. Progress stored on your device stays until you reset it or delete the app.
