Scoring

Closer scores more

Every card is worth up to 1,000 points. The curve is steep near the market price and forgiving further out, so a sharp eye beats a lucky guess. A bullseye within 2% takes nearly all of it. Within 20% still earns two thirds.

  • Bullseyeunder 2%988+
  • Incredible2–5%953–988
  • Very close5–10%872–953
  • Good10–20%678–872
  • Close20–30%490–678
  • Off30–50%216–490
  • Way off50%+under 216

Market IQ

Skill, not grind

Market IQ is a rating out of 1,000 that measures how accurately you price cards. It follows your recent guesses, so it climbs as your eye sharpens, and playing more won't lift it on its own.

  • IQRated on your Classic and Daily guessesPacks and Screener games are for fun: they earn XP but leave your rating alone.
  • XPLevels and streaksEvery game earns XP. Play the Daily each day to keep your streak going.
  • SYNYours on every deviceSign in with Apple or Google to sync your progress and join the leaderboard.

FAQ

Good questions

Is CardIQ free?

Yes. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where do the prices come from?

Prices are UK market values from PokePulse, refreshed daily. Each reveal also shows US and Cardmarket prices where available and, once enough history has built up, which way the price has moved.

Which cards are in it?

{{ show.stats.cards }} English-language Pokémon cards from {{ show.stats.sets }} sets, released between {{ show.stats.firstYear }} and {{ show.stats.lastYear }}. Classic and the Daily deal cards worth between £5 and £1,000.

When does the Daily reset?

At midnight UK time. Everyone gets the same five cards, and each guess is locked in before the price is revealed.

Do I need an account?

No. Every mode works as a guest. Sign in with Apple or Google if you want the Daily leaderboard or to sync your progress between devices.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. CardIQ is launching on iPhone first.
